I’ve written to you four times now and asked for nothing. This is the one where I ask.

What I offer is called a Virtual Tea. It is a free, unhurried thirty-minute conversation — no pressure, no pitch. Just a quiet space to be heard and find one clear next step.

Sometimes that step is something we do together. Often it isn’t. Some of the better conversations I’ve had ended with me saying that the honest answer was rest, or a doctor, or nothing at all for six months.

I keep them few on purpose. I’d rather have four real conversations in a week than forty polite ones.

So — if you’d like to talk, the link is below. And if you’d rather keep reading quietly from a distance, that is a complete answer. It is what most people do, and I’ll keep writing either way.

— Rachel
