The part I should probably mention

Rachel Mense   ·   20 August 2026

  ◆  

There’s something I’d rather tell you myself than have you find out later.

The essential oils I use and guide people with come from Young Living, and the cellular products from ASEA. Both are direct-selling companies — network marketing. If you enrol through me, I earn something on it.

I know how that sounds, because I know what people find when they look those companies up. The honest version: most people who sign up as distributors make almost nothing.

Which is exactly why I don’t lead with the business side, and why I’d genuinely rather you just used the products — or didn’t. What I actually do is the guidance: the rituals, the practice, the part that takes twelve years to learn and about ten minutes a day to live.

If knowing that changes things for you, I completely understand, and there’s an unsubscribe link below that I won’t take personally.

If it doesn’t, I’ll write again in a few days with something more interesting.

— Rachel
